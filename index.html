What is The Small Talk Project?

The Small Talk Project is a civic hospitality initiative built around brief, intentional conversations held in borrowed public spaces.

There is no debate stage. No performance. No pressure to solve everything.

Just a table, an invitation, a welcome, and a shared moment of conversation.

Would you like to be seated?

We are currently gathering names for our first pilot conversations.

Participants will be invited to sit for a brief, guided conversation in a welcoming public space. You do not need to prepare anything. You do not need to arrive with an argument, a cause, or a perfect story.

You only need to bring your presence.

  • A simple arrival
  • A seated conversation
  • Two folded cards: an invitation and a welcome
  • A brief shared prompt
  • A closing gift
  • Approximately 30 minutes of conversation

Do you have a space to offer?

The Small Talk Project is currently seeking possible host spaces for pilot gatherings.

A borrowed civic sanctuary can be a café, coffee shop, restaurant, bookstore, gallery, community space, courtyard, or other public setting willing to hold a small moment of intentional conversation.

We are looking for spaces that can offer quiet, beauty, welcome, and a little room for people to sit across from one another.

  • 4–6 participants
  • Seated in pairs
  • Approximately 30 minutes of conversation
  • Around one hour of space usage
  • Minimal setup
  • No stage, microphone, or special equipment required

What happens at the table?

At each table, participants receive two folded cards.

One is an invitation. One is a welcome.

The cards are exchanged, read silently, then read aloud to one another. After the cards have been received, the conversation begins.

The purpose is not to impress, persuade, or perform.

The purpose is to practice presence, respect, and curiosity.
