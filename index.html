Pilot Season

We are preparing the first tables.

The Small Talk Project is beginning with intentionally small pilot gatherings for early guests and possible host spaces.

We are currently welcoming people who would like to be seated, spaces that may be willing to host, and community partners who believe conversation still matters.

Small gatherings. Seated in pairs. Approximately thirty minutes.

No debate stage. No performance. No pressure to solve everything.

Each gathering is held with presence, respect, and curiosity.

The Invitation

Begin with a note.

You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out.

You may be writing because you would like to be seated, because you have a space to offer, or because something about the project resonated with you.

Share only what helps us respond with care.

Optional, but helpful if you are interested in being seated.

If you are offering a space, please include the name of the space and where it is located. If you are hoping to be seated, please share anything that would help us receive you well.

Prefer email? You may also write to hello@smalltalkproject.com.
